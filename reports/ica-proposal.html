Constraints and failure modes

Multiple directions

Whiten z, then constrain WWᵀ = I.
This yields k ≤ d orthogonal directions in a d-dimensional subspace.

High kurtosis can be misleading

Rare outliers or positional effects can dominate the fourth moment.
Use held-out activations, both signs, and counterfactual checks.

Capacity and causal limits

ICA has fewer directions than an overcomplete SAE.
A readable label does not explain an entire circuit.

Non-Gaussianity is a discovery signal. Its semantic and causal value needs independent evidence. Positive excess kurtosis is one measure of heavy tails, not a complete measure of all non-Gaussianity.
